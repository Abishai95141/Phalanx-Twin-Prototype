## Calibrate now (priority subset, AD-55 item 6)

1. **Ground-idle acceleration margin** (W2.5 finding; top priority, because it makes engines roll back).
   - Steady idle with M09's idle loads (≈21 kW PTO, 0.5 kg/s bleed) needs Wf/PS3 of 1.20–1.36 × M08's reference
     line, against `acc_k` 1.4 × a line derived from the no-load engine.
   - After a small sag, deteriorated engines, and 2 of 5 healthy identities, roll back to WF_MIN with T5 rising.
   - **Fix:** derive the reference and acceleration schedules from the loaded engine; keep the released accel
     timing; justify the margin with evidence.
   - Turn the strict xfail in `tests/integration/test_faults_bench_trim.py` green.
2. **Steady levels and operating line:**
   - C1–C3, C5, C6, C14;
   - the idle anchors, MIL Wf, MIL at ≈95 % N2 (AD-46), closed-loop IRP;
   - **SLS idle and part-power T5.** The low-power T5 calibration also unblocks relight-by-T5 and the deferred
     analytic check.
3. **Transient timing:**
   - C17, C18 small-step, C22 run-down;
   - accel vs decel τ (decel slower; `docs/behavioural_targets.md` B3 ii structure);
   - transient-weighted identification (AD-34).
4. **Fan lag** (AD-52 primary mechanism). On the Tejas loop N1–N2 is 0.84–0.86 and the N2→N1 phase only
   −9…−12° at ≈0.18 Hz.
   - Calibrate the LP rotor dynamics (inertia and fan torque characteristics) to IN20/F404 evidence (fan
     small-step τ, spool time constants).
   - Report the N2→N1 phase/gain/coherence per sub-band and the B1-fast N1–N2. Only the **structure**
     transfers from the proxy; magnitudes come from IN20 evidence or sweeps.
5. **EGT level and law:** the corrected-temperature exponent (structure 0.73), and EGT phase vs N1 (AD-52 item 3;
   EGT must lag N1).
6. **Fault-signature magnitudes** (W2.5 B7 D3 rerun; Muir convention per AD-53):
   - fan fouling N2c vs N1c +0.16 % (Muir: down);
   - HPC fouling fuel vs N2 +0.51 % (Muir: down);
   - HPC erosion pair-4 roll-back (item 1);
   - HPT PS3/P1 vs N2 −0.34 % (up), N2c vs P56/P1 0 (down);
   - **C26** PS3/P1 +2.37 % against 6–10 %.

   Fix them through the component maps and deterioration modifiers, never by direct output edits.
7. **C12 max-AB** nozzle area and AB fuel at −10.9 % (W2.1b strict xfail).
8. **T1 at the span floor:** residual N1c 1.049–1.050 against the 1.044 cap at 11 km, PLA 90.
9. **Nozzle-fault limit cycle:** 0.6 Hz against the catalogue's 1–2 Hz (check the catalogue evidence).
10. **C28 precision floor** (W2.1c item 9): Henry's figures are snapshot repeatability. Do not raise sensor noise
    above the measured noise ceiling (`docs/behavioural_targets.md`).
11. **Usage-mode engine surrogate:** a PWL/LPV surrogate built from the calibrated CLM, with an **error envelope**
    against it over the operating envelope. It replaces the W1 usage-mode engine stand-ins, so usage mode and
    telemetry mode stay consistent at sortie-summary level (AD-10).

**Deferred (strict xfails, each keeping its measured number and the reason "AD-55 deferred"):**

- C11 (test-design defect);
- C15 (VG convention, AD-53);
- C24;
- C20/21 and C23 (structural PROXY; the surge model at fixed fuel over-speeds to ≈125 % N2 open-loop).

## Acceptance

| ID | Criterion |
|---|---|
| A1 | Every calibrate-now item is green within its tolerance (±3 % steady, ±25 % transient timing; tolerances never tighter than real repeatability), or a strict xfail with the measured number and a reason; no bound widened |
| A2 | The ground-idle roll-back is gone: the W2.5 trim xfail is green; healthy and deteriorated idle are stable under the M09 loads |
| A3 | Fan-lag structure: N2→N1 phase lag clearly increased toward the evidence, with N1–N2 coherence < 1 and FF correlation signs still positive below the N1-limited region (AD-50) |
| A4 | Surrogate error envelope stated over the operating envelope (n stated); usage/telemetry consistency test |
| A5 | Tejas golden, W1.3a/W1.3b, W2.1b and proxy tests stay green; determinism and complex-step paths intact |
