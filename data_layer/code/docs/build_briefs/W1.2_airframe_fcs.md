## Deliverables

### M06: airframe
- **Dynamics:** quaternion 6-DOF rigid body with fixed-step integration inside the 240 Hz step.
- **Ground model:** gear, ground reaction, brakes and brake energy (`af.brake_energy_rate`), WoW per leg.
- **Tejas-anchored aero build-up (AD-08):**
  - longitudinal core fitted to the Tejas lift curve, pitch derivatives, trim points and instability map;
  - lateral and nonlinear shapes from ADMIRE/GAM and F-106B;
  - Mach trends from DATCOM/VSPAERO, with vortex lift added;
  - build-up drag;
  - per-surface tables, including hinge moments.
- **Mass properties** come from `util.mass/cg/inertia` (M09). Reference inertia 14,088 / 66,559 / 77,875 / 2,491 kg·m² at ≈9 t, CG 34 % MAC.

### M05: FCS
**Quadruplex DFCC replica:**
- 80 Hz frame, 8 ms computation delay, median voting;
- gain scheduling on Mach and altitude;
- AoA and g limiters;
- reversionary law with WoW/undercarriage-dependent gains;
- CBIT/PBIT/MBIT hooks over 23 subsystems per channel. These are event emission points; M14 consumes them.

**13 actuators** (dual-hydraulic primaries at 280 bar):
- ~9 Hz bandwidth, with rate and position limits;
- **rate capability ∝ √(ΔP_avail − ΔP_load)**, with `util.hyd_dp_avail` as input;
- flow demand Q = A·ẋ per system;
- consumes `health.actuator`.

**Inputs:** sensor lanes from the in-loop observation path (`W1_interfaces.md` §3). Bind them as `lane.<channel_id>`, in SI. In W1 tests, use a labelled test-fixture lane source.
