## Deliverables

### 1. Control law (per engine, vectorised over `n_engines`)
MIN/MAX selection of schedules and limiters, per the F404 structure:
- **Below IRP:**
  - N2 scheduled on PLA/T25;
  - A8 scheduled on PLA;
  - PS3 overrides.
- **At and above IRP:**
  - N1 isochronous vs T1, with a 104.4 % corrected cap;
  - A8 modulated to hold T5 = f(T1, P0).
- **Accel/decel limits** on Wf/PS3.
- **Geometry and afterburner:**
  - VG schedules;
  - AB ratio-unit fuel with nozzle pre-open.
- **Protection:**
  - flameout detection and auto-relight;
  - hung-start monitor.

`fadec.active_limiter` reports which branch is selected.

### 2. Closed-loop governing (AD-31)
- The loop acts every FADEC frame on **measured** lane values.
- Process disturbances produce small-signal Wf corrections that **lag** the speed excursion. Commanded steps still **lead** with fuel.
- Loop gains and bandwidth are tagged parameters with sweeps; W2.4 identifies them on proxy data.
- **Proxy-mode autothrottle loop:** `pilot.at_engage`, `pilot.at_n1_cmd` → N1 command, as a parameterised outer loop.

### 3. Signal validation template
- Range checks with n consecutive frames.
- Rate checks over 4 frames.
- Cross-channel checks: take the safer value plus soft-fail; average when the channels agree.
- Analytical comparison with model substitution: 25 % steady / 45 % transient tolerance; heal after 10 good frames.
- Accommodation that changes the plant: inhibit AB, force actuators safe, reduce the N2 limit by ≈1,000 rpm.
- **Deliberately injectable validator failure modes** (tagged config):
  - tolerance too wide;
  - scaling wrap;
  - persistence too long;
  - skew + noise false failures;
  - common-mode faults.

### 4. Timing and in-loop sensing
- FADEC runs on its own clock domain. Commands apply at the next truth frame.
- Lane inputs come from the in-loop observation path (`W1_interfaces.md` §3).
- In W1 tests, use labelled test fixtures for the plant (a first/second-order spool model) and for the lanes.
